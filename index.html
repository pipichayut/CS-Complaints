How this was measured
  • Source: customer messages from the Virtual AI assistant log (Thai and English), read from the published Google Sheet each time the page opens, or from a file loaded on the page. The page keeps a saved copy in your browser so it opens quickly.
  • Each message was tagged with Thai and English keyword rules per scenario (for example "ยังไม่ได้รับ", "ไม่ครบ", "ถูกยกเลิก", "ยังไม่ได้เงินคืน", "โค้ดใช้ไม่ได้", "otp", "โทรไม่ติด", "not received", "refund"). Rules were spot-checked against random samples and tuned to remove common false matches, such as customers who ordered the wrong size themselves.
  • A "complaint" message matches at least one scenario or uses strong negative wording. Cancel, return/exchange and "talk to a human" requests are shown separately as friction requests.
  • A "chat" is one Session ID, counted on the day it started. "Sent to call center" means the bot's reply contained a phone number, form link, email address or a referral to staff.
  • Deep-dive causes use the customer's words plus the order status the bot found in the same chat. The example quotes in each deep dive come from the April to September 2026 analysis.
  • Complaint watch: each scenario's total for the time frame is compared with the previous period of the same length. A spike day is a day at least 1.5 times the 7-day average and 8 or more messages above it.
  • Keyword tagging is an estimate. Treat counts as directional, accurate for comparing scenarios and periods rather than exact totals.
